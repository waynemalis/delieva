At Botanas Delieva, we use cookies and similar technologies to improve our users' browsing experience.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on a user's device while browsing a website.

They are used to remember preferences and collect statistical information about browsing activity.

## 2. Types of cookies we use

### Essential cookies

Required for the basic functionality of the website.

### Performance cookies

Help us analyze how users interact with the website to improve content and functionality.

### Functional cookies

Allow us to remember user preferences.

### Third-party cookies

Some external services, such as analytics tools or social media platforms, may use their own cookies.

## 3. Purpose of cookie usage

Cookies may be used to:

- Improve website speed and performance
- Analyze traffic and browsing behavior
- Remember preferences
- Optimize advertising campaigns or content

## 4. Cookie management

Users may configure their browser to:

- Block cookies
- Delete stored cookies
- Receive alerts before accepting cookies

> Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

## 5. Changes to this policy

Botanas Delieva may update this Cookie Policy at any time.

Any changes will be published on this page.
