Our History

Delieva Snacks was born in the community of Las Puentes, municipality of Meoqui in the great state of Chihuahua. With a long tradition in food production and transformation, Delieva Snacks specializes in the preparation of snacks based on peanuts, nuts, dried fruits, and popcorn. Our history begins with the vision of Don José Soto Cisneros and the launch in partnership with his family, who decided to start a family business that would offer high-quality products with an unparalleled taste. Over time, we have expanded our variety of snacks to offer more than 40 options, developed to adapt to different tastes and consumption moments. Today our goal is to continue growing and taking our snacks to new markets, sharing a taste experience that reflects our passion for what we do.

Mission

To offer high-quality snacks made with selected ingredients from the central-southern region of Chihuahua, combining tradition and innovation to provide authentic flavors that create a delicious experience and satisfy all our customers' tastes.

Vision

To be a leading snack brand at the state and national level, recognized for its quality, variety of flavors and safety, taking its products to more sales points and consolidating itself as the favorite option for infant, young and adult consumers.
